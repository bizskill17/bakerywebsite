About Bliss Bites

Made with Love,
Baked with Happiness

Welcome to Bliss Bites by Nutan Jain, a home-based bakery in Guwahati, where a passion for baking turns simple ingredients into delightful treats.

At Bliss Bites, we believe that happiness can be found in the little things, especially in a delicious homemade treat shared with someone you love.

From our rich, fudgy brownies and indulgent brownie cakes to our freshly baked dry cakes, every creation is prepared with love, care and attention to detail.

Whether you're celebrating a special occasion, surprising someone you love or simply treating yourself, we're here to make your moments a little sweeter.

Because at Bliss Bites,
every bite brings a smile!
